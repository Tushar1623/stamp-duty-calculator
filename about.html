About StampDutyCalc.in

Demystifying Indian property registration parameters cleanly.

Who We Are

StampDutyCalc.in is an independent, free-to-use financial calculator tool built specifically for property buyers and sellers in West Bengal. We are not a government website and are not affiliated with the Directorate of Registration & Stamp Revenue (IGR West Bengal) or any other government body.

Our Mission

Navigating stamp duty, registration fees, and circle rates in West Bengal is notoriously complex. Our mission is to demystify this process — giving every property buyer, regardless of background or budget, access to clear, accurate, and up-to-date information before they visit the sub-registrar's office.

Who Builds This Tool

StampDutyCalc.in is developed and maintained by a West Bengal-based real estate developer with hands-on experience in IGR WB compliance, circle rate zones, HIDCO/NKDA property transactions, and sub-registrar office workflows across Kolkata, New Town, Howrah, and other districts. Rate data is manually verified against official gazette notifications and the IGR portal.

What We Are Not

We are not a law firm, a registered document writer, or a financial advisory service. The estimates produced by this calculator are indicative only. Always verify final stamp duty and registration fees with a licensed Deed Writer or at the official IGR West Bengal portal (wbregistration.gov.in) before executing any property transaction.

How We Fund This Site

StampDutyCalc.in is a free service supported by Google AdSense advertising. We do not charge users for calculator access. Ads are served by Google and are subject to Google's advertising policies.
