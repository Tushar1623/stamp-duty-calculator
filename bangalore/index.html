Complete Guide to Bengaluru & Karnataka Stamp Duty (2025–2026)

Mastering Kaveri 2.0 guidance values, BBMP cesses, urban surcharges, A-Khata vs B-Khata considerations, and registration outlays in Bengaluru.

1. Karnataka's Slabs and Statutory Surcharges Explained

Under the Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957, stamp duty on sale deeds is computed through a tiered slab structure supplemented by mandatory local authority cesses:

  • Affordable Bracket (Up to ₹20 Lakhs): 2.0% Base Stamp Duty + 10% Cess + 2% Urban Surcharge = 2.24% duty + 2% Registration Fee = 4.24% total.
  • Mid Bracket (₹20,00,001 to ₹45 Lakhs): 3.0% Base Stamp Duty + 10% Cess + 2% Urban Surcharge = 3.36% duty + 2% Registration Fee = 5.36% total.
  • Standard Bracket (Above ₹45 Lakhs): 5.0% Base Stamp Duty + 10% Cess + 2% Urban Surcharge = 5.60% duty (often cited as ~5.55% in historical models) + 2% Registration Fee = ~7.60% total.

Note on Surcharges: The 10% Infrastructure Cess and 2% Urban Surcharge are calculated as percentages of the base stamp duty amount, not the entire property value. (e.g. 10% of 5% = 0.50%; 2% of 5% = 0.10%).

2. Guidance Value vs. Agreement Value in Karnataka

Under Section 45-A of the Karnataka Stamp Act, duties are mandatorily assessed on the higher of the actual consideration declared in the deed or the official government Guidance Value published on the Kaveri Online Services portal (Kaveri 2.0). If you buy a flat in Whitefield for ₹90 Lakhs, but the notified guidance rate for the built-up area and super-built-up area comes to ₹95 Lakhs, stamp duty and registration fees must be remitted on ₹95 Lakhs.

3. Family Gift Deeds in Bengaluru (Article 28 Concession)

When gifting immovable property to immediate family members (Parents, Spouse, Children, Siblings, Grandchildren), Karnataka levies a concessional fixed tariff instead of ad-valorem percentages:

  • BBMP / Bengaluru City Corporation: Fixed base duty of ₹5,000 + ₹500 cess + ₹100 surcharge = ₹5,600 total duty, plus a fixed registration fee of ₹1,000 = ₹6,600 net outgo.
  • Town / City Municipal Councils: Fixed base duty of ₹3,000 (+ cesses = ₹3,360) + ₹1,000 registration fee = ₹4,360.
  • Gram Panchayat / Rural Limits: Fixed base duty of ₹1,000 (+ cesses = ₹1,130) + ₹1,000 registration fee = ₹2,130.

4. Bengaluru Micro-Market Overview & Khata Types

Key locality hubs across Bengaluru urban require careful verification of Khata status before scheduling SRO appointments:

  • East Bengaluru (IT Corridor): Whitefield, Marathahalli, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, Varthur, Outer Ring Road (ORR). High commercial density and major residential high-rises.
  • South Bengaluru: Koramangala, HSR Layout, BTM Layout, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, Electronic City. Mature residential pockets with premium guidance values.
  • North Bengaluru: Hebbal, Yelahanka, Thanisandra, Devanahalli, Airport Road. High appreciation corridor driven by airport infrastructure and tech parks.
  • Khata Clarity: An A-Khata property signifies full municipal compliance with BBMP building bylaws, while a B-Khata property is an entry in the property tax register without formal occupancy completion, subject to specific bank lending restrictions.
