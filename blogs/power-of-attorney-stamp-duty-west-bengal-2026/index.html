Power of Attorney Stamp Duty West Bengal 2026: Builder PoA Slabs, General PoA Charges and Registration

📅 Last Reviewed: October 2026
⏱️ Reading Time: 10 min read
🏛️ Jurisdiction: West Bengal

In West Bengal, a power of attorney given to a promoter or developer to construct on, sell or transfer immovable property pays fixed stamp duty by the market value of the property, from ₹5,000 for property up to ₹30 Lakhs to ₹75,000 above ₹3 Crore. That is a fraction of the 6% to 7% a sale deed costs, but a PoA does not transfer ownership, so it is no substitute for one.

A landowner in Kolkata hands a developer a general power of attorney so the builder can sign sale agreements with flat buyers. A buyer in Howrah is told that a PoA from the owner is "as good as a sale deed" and costs a few thousand rupees. The first is a standard, regulated arrangement. The second is a trap, and the Supreme Court has said so.

This guide sets out the stamp duty slabs for a builder PoA in West Bengal, what a PoA can and cannot do, how it compares with the cost of a registered sale, and how to register one.

Quick answer (2026): For a PoA to a promoter or developer for construction, sale or transfer of immovable property, duty is fixed by the market value of the property: ₹5,000 (up to ₹30 Lakhs), ₹7,000 (₹30 to ₹60 Lakhs), ₹10,000 (₹60 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore), ₹20,000 (₹1 to ₹1.5 Crore), ₹40,000 (₹1.5 to ₹3 Crore) and ₹75,000 (above ₹3 Crore). Where full duty has already been paid on a developer's agreement between the same parties for the same property, the PoA duty is reported to fall to ₹50.

1. The Legal Framework

A power of attorney is an instrument under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, taxed under Article 48 of Schedule 1A as it applies in West Bengal. The state amended Article 48 by the West Bengal Finance Act, 2012, which introduced value-based duty for powers of attorney given to promoters and developers in respect of immovable property.

Three other laws shape how a PoA works in practice.

  • The Powers of Attorney Act, 1882 deals with the execution and proof of such instruments.
  • The Registration Act, 1908 governs registration. A PoA that authorises a person to deal in immovable property is usually registered so that it is accepted by registrars and third parties.
  • The Transfer of Property Act, 1882 governs what is needed to transfer ownership. A PoA is not a transfer.

The practical rule of thumb is that the PoA is a mandate to act for the owner, while a registered sale deed is the transfer itself.

2. Stamp Duty Slabs for a Builder PoA

The table below follows the state's published Schedule 1A extract for Article 48 clause (g), a PoA given to a promoter or developer, by whatever name called, for construction on, or sale of, or transfer in any manner of any immovable property.

Market value of the property Stamp duty on the PoA
Up to ₹30 Lakhs ₹5,000
Above ₹30 Lakhs, up to ₹60 Lakhs ₹7,000
Above ₹60 Lakhs, up to ₹1 Crore ₹10,000
Above ₹1 Crore, up to ₹1.5 Crore ₹20,000
Above ₹1.5 Crore, up to ₹3 Crore ₹40,000
Above ₹3 Crore ₹75,000

Several points matter.

  • The slab follows market value, not your price. The market value is the figure computed by the registration department's valuation tools.
  • The same six slabs apply to a developer's agreement. An agreement giving a promoter or developer authority to construct on, sell or transfer property is taxed under Article 5 with identical slab amounts.
  • The ₹50 rule. The explanation to the clause reports that where the proper stamp duty has been paid on a promoter's or developer's agreement between the same parties for the same property, the duty on the PoA is ₹50. This prevents double charging when a landowner signs both documents.
  • The registration fee is separate and is shown on your e-Assessment slip.

3. Other Kinds of PoA

Article 48 has more than one clause. The builder PoA slabs above apply to a specific category. A PoA of another kind falls under its own clause, with its own, generally lower, duty. Typical examples include a PoA to appear in court, to collect rent, to operate a bank account, to manage property or to represent the donor at a registration without power to sell.

Because the state's extract of the schedule shows only part of the article (it notes that there are other clauses), confirm the duty for your specific type of PoA on the portal or with the Sub-Registrar. Do not apply the builder slabs to a PoA that is not for construction, sale or transfer of property.

General PoA vs special PoA. A general power of attorney (GPA) authorises the agent to act broadly. A special power of attorney (SPA) is limited to a specified act or transaction. The duty depends on the clause the document falls under, not on the label.

4. What a PoA Can and Cannot Do

A PoA can

  • Authorise a developer to sign agreements and receive payments on the owner's behalf
  • Allow an agent to manage, let or maintain the property
  • Let an NRI owner transact through someone in India
  • Allow an agent to execute and register a sale deed in the owner's name, where the PoA expressly permits it

A PoA cannot

  • Transfer ownership by itself
  • Substitute for a registered sale deed
  • Survive the principal's death in most cases, because the agency generally ends when the principal dies

In the Suraj Lamp & Industries decision (2011), the Supreme Court held that transactions by way of general power of attorney, agreement to sell and will do not amount to valid transfers of title in immovable property. A buyer who relies on a GPA sale and never gets a registered conveyance has not acquired title in the way the law requires. For a buyer, the question to ask is whether the end result is a registered sale deed, not a stack of PoAs.

5. Registration and the Online Process

The process runs through wbregistration.gov.in.

Step 1: Identify the correct clause. Confirm whether the PoA falls under the developer clause or another clause of Article 48.

Step 2: Check the market value. For a builder PoA, the slab depends on the market value of the property covered. Use the land or flat tool on the portal.

Step 3: File the e-Requisition. Select the deed type as a power of attorney and enter the principal, the agent and the property.

Step 4: Download the e-Assessment slip. It shows the duty and the registration fee, with a 14-digit query number and year.

Step 5: Pay through GRIPS. Use net banking, SBI ePay, debit card or UPI. Online payment is compulsory where the amount is ₹5 Lakhs or more.

Step 6: Appear before the Registrar. The principal appears in person with ID and witnesses. A PoA executed abroad has additional authentication requirements, so take advice if the principal is an NRI.

6. Worked Examples

Both examples assume a municipal area, a 1% registration fee on sale values and the slabs in Section 2.

Example A: ₹45 Lakh property

A landowner gives a developer a PoA covering a plot with a market value of ₹45 Lakhs.

Document Duty Registration fee Total
PoA to developer (₹30 to ₹60 Lakh slab) ₹7,000 See slip ₹7,000 plus fee
Registered sale deed (6%) ₹2,70,000 ₹45,000 ₹3,15,000

The PoA costs a small fraction of the sale deed, but the owner still holds title until a sale deed is executed.

Example B: ₹1.2 Crore property

A PoA covers property with a market value of ₹1.2 Crore.

Document Duty Registration fee Total
PoA to developer (₹1 to ₹1.5 Crore slab) ₹20,000 See slip ₹20,000 plus fee
Registered sale deed (7%) ₹8,40,000 ₹1,20,000 ₹9,60,000

The duty difference is ₹8,20,000 before fees. That is the saving some sellers and buyers are tempted by, and it is exactly the structure the Supreme Court said does not transfer title.

Example C: A slab edge

Market value Slab Duty
₹99,00,000 ₹60 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore ₹10,000
₹1,01,00,000 ₹1 Crore to ₹1.5 Crore ₹20,000

A ₹2 Lakh increase in value across a slab edge doubles the duty. Slab edges are hard thresholds, so check the assessed value, not your price.

7. Gift Deed and Lease, Briefly, and Tax Savings

This is a brief outline. Separate guides cover each deed in depth.

  • Gift deed. A gift to a member of the donor's family pays 0.5% of market value as stamp duty. A gift to anyone else pays the conveyance rate (6% in municipal areas, 5% in panchayat areas, plus 1% additional above ₹1 Crore). Registration fee about 1%.
  • Lease. For a lease at a fixed rent with no premium, the duty depends on the term. Up to one year, it is the same duty as a bond on the whole amount payable. For more than one and up to ten years, it is the conveyance duty on twice the average annual rent. For more than ten and up to thirty years, it is the conveyance duty on three times the average annual rent. Beyond thirty years, it is the conveyance duty on the market value of the property. Leases with a premium or advance carry additional duty on that amount.

Section 80C. Stamp duty and registration charges on the purchase of a residential property can be claimed as a deduction within the ₹1.5 Lakh overall limit in the year of payment, under the old tax regime. That deduction relates to the purchase. Whether duty on a separate PoA qualifies is a question for your tax adviser. Income-tax law was reorganised in 2026, so confirm the section reference before filing.

8. Documents Checklist and Common Errors

For the registration appointment

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the principal and the agent
  • Draft PoA stating the powers precisely, the property description and the period
  • Title documents of the principal, including the chain of title
  • Latest municipal tax receipt and mutation record
  • For a builder PoA: the development agreement and any sanctioned plan
  • e-Assessment slip and GRIPS challan
  • Two witnesses with ID and photographs
  • For an NRI principal: passport, overseas address proof and the authentication required for a document signed abroad

Common errors

  1. Treating a PoA as a way to avoid sale deed duty.
  2. Applying the builder slabs to a PoA that falls under a different clause.
  3. Using price instead of market value to pick the slab.
  4. Paying full PoA duty when the ₹50 rule for a developer's agreement between the same parties may apply.
  5. Drafting vague powers, which registrars and banks may refuse to accept.
  6. Assuming the PoA survives the principal's death.
  7. Not registering a PoA that is meant to be used with registrars.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the stamp duty on a power of attorney in West Bengal?

For a PoA to a promoter or developer for construction, sale or transfer of immovable property, it is a fixed amount by market value: ₹5,000 up to ₹30 Lakhs, rising to ₹75,000 above ₹3 Crore. Other kinds of PoA fall under other clauses of Article 48.

What is the builder PoA stamp duty in West Bengal for a ₹1.2 Crore property?

₹20,000, because ₹1.2 Crore falls in the ₹1 Crore to ₹1.5 Crore slab. The registration fee is separate and shows on your e-Assessment slip.

Is a general power of attorney enough to buy or sell property?

No. The Supreme Court held in Suraj Lamp & Industries (2011) that a GPA-based transaction does not by itself transfer title to immovable property. A registered sale deed is needed.

Does the PoA duty depend on my price or the market value?

On the market value of the property, as assessed through the registration department's valuation tools.

Does a PoA need to be registered?

A PoA that authorises dealing with immovable property is usually registered so that registrars and third parties accept it, and it is taxed under the stamp duty schedule either way. Confirm the requirement for your purpose with the Sub-Registrar.

Can the PoA duty be reduced if I also sign a developer's agreement?

The schedule's explanation reports that where the proper duty has been paid on a promoter's or developer's agreement between the same parties for the same property, the duty on the PoA is ₹50. Confirm how it applies to your documents.
