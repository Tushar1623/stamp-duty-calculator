📋 Submitting Rate Correction Requests

We are deeply committed to maintaining data accuracy across our calculation engines for West Bengal, Maharashtra, and Delhi. If you believe a stamp duty rate, surcharge threshold, circle rate, or registration fee on our platform is outdated or differs from newly notified rules, please email us with:

  • State & Locality: The specific state, municipality, district, or mouza/ward.
  • Identified Discrepancy: The exact formula, percentage, or threshold you noticed.
  • Official Source Link: A reference link or copy of the state government gazette notification or official revenue portal link (e.g. from IGR WB, IGR Maharashtra, or Delhi Revenue).

Our editorial team manually verifies incoming correction requests against official gazettes before updating our calculation algorithms.
