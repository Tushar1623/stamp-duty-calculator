📋 Rate Correction Requests

If you believe a circle rate, Ready Reckoner rate, or state stamp duty figure on our calculators is outdated or incorrect, please email us with the following details so we can cross-verify and patch it immediately:

  • The specific state and area/locality/mouza.
  • The exact rate or threshold discrepancy you identified.
  • A reference link or document screenshot from the official state portal (IGR West Bengal or IGR Maharashtra) if available.

We manually verify all incoming correction data against official government gazette notifications before pushing updates live to the application.
