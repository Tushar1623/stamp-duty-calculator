Complete Guide to Delhi Property Stamp Duty & MCD Transfer Duty (2025–2026)

Comprehensive overview of Delhi circle rate categories A through H, female buyer rebates, spousal joint ownership, and e-stamping via NGDRS.

1. Understanding Delhi's Dual-Component Stamp Duty

Property registrations in the National Capital Territory of Delhi involve two distinct statutory components administered under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957:

  • State Stamp Duty: 3% for Male buyers, 2% for Sole Female buyers (200 bps statutory rebate), and 2.5% for joint male-female spousal ownership.
  • MCD Transfer Duty: Levied by the Municipal Corporation of Delhi. Under the revised July 10, 2023 Gazette Notification, properties valued above ₹25 Lakhs attract 4% for male buyers and 3% for female buyers (3.5% joint). For properties valued up to ₹25 Lakhs, the rate is 3% for men and 2% for women.
  • Registration Fee: Flat 1% of property valuation + standard ₹100 pasting fee.

2. Delhi Circle Rate Categorization (Categories A to H)

The Government of NCT of Delhi categorizes all residential colonies into eight valuation tiers (A through H) for minimum land and construction valuation:

  • Category A (Highest Baseline): Maharani Bagh, Friends Colony, Panchsheel Park, Vasant Vihar, Golf Links, Shanti Niketan.
  • Category B: Greater Kailash (GK I & II), Defence Colony, Hauz Khas, Green Park, Anand Niketan, Gulmohar Park.
  • Category C: Vasant Kunj, Alaknanda, East of Kailash, Chittaranjan Park (CR Park), Kalkaji.
  • Category D: Dwarka sectors, Mayur Vihar, Saket, Janakpuri, Lajpat Nagar, Karol Bagh.
  • Categories E to H: Rohini sectors, Dilshad Garden, Uttam Nagar, Burari, Narela, outer Delhi sub-colonies.

Valuation Formula: Total Circle Valuation = Land Value (Area × Notified Colony Rate) + Construction Value (Plinth Area × Minimum Construction Rate × Age Depreciation Factor).

3. Gift Deeds in Delhi: Legal Realities

Unlike certain jurisdictions that provide nominal token charges for family transfers, Delhi levies standard conveyance stamp duty on Gift Deeds based on the gender of the donee (recipient):

  • Gift deed to a female recipient: 5% Stamp Duty + 1% Reg Fee = 6% Total (in MCD > ₹25L).
  • Gift deed to a male recipient: 7% Stamp Duty + 1% Reg Fee = 8% Total.
  • Income Tax Exemption: While state stamp duty does not grant a bloodline waiver, gifts of immovable property between defined "relatives" under Section 56(2)(x) of the Income Tax Act are 100% exempt from income tax in the hands of the recipient.
