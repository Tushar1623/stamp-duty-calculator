1. For Informational & Educational Purposes Only

The stamp duty, registration charges, circle rates, and municipal cess estimates provided by StampDutyCalc.in are developed for general informational and educational guidance only. They are designed to assist property buyers, sellers, and advocates in understanding prevailing statutory rate structures across West Bengal, Maharashtra, and Delhi.

This website does not provide formal legal, tax, surveying, conveyancing, or financial advice.

2. No Guarantee of Valuation Accuracy

While our team makes every effort to verify formulas and rate thresholds against published state gazette notifications, the actual statutory charges payable on any property transaction are determined exclusively by the concerned Sub-Registrar’s Office (SRO / DSR / ADSR) at the time of deed execution.

Discrepancies may arise due to:

  • Specific mouza, ward, or street-level circle rate variations that require physical inspection or Cadastral mapping.
  • Road width loadings, floor-rise multipliers, or specific property classifications (e.g., Bastu vs. Sali land in West Bengal).
  • Recent amendments by state finance departments that have not yet been reflected in digital databases.
  • Specific conditions attached to exemptions, concessions, or power of attorney instruments.

3. Mandatory Verification with Official Sources

Users are strongly advised to verify final assessment figures and obtain official e-Assessment slips through the designated state registration portals or via a licensed Deed Writer prior to financial disbursements:

  • West Bengal: Directorate of Registration & Stamp Revenue (wbregistration.gov.in)
  • Maharashtra: Inspector General of Registration (igrmaharashtra.gov.in)
  • Delhi (NCT): Revenue Department, Government of NCT of Delhi (revenue.delhi.gov.in)
