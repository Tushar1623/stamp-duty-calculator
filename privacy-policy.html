Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026  |  Website: StampDutyCalc.in

At StampDutyCalc.in, we are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how that information is utilized, and your choices regarding cookies, tracking, and advertising technologies.

1. Client-Side Calculations (Zero Personal Financial Data Collected)

StampDutyCalc.in is architected as a 100% client-side web application. All financial parameters you enter into our calculators — including property purchase prices, circle rates, area in square feet, gender selections, and deed instrument types — are processed exclusively inside your web browser using local JavaScript.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any property financial data you enter into our calculators. When you refresh or close your browser tab, your inputs are cleared immediately.

2. Web Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-LQG97FB2GP) to analyze website traffic, understand how users navigate through our calculators and educational guides, and optimize site speed and performance. Google Analytics collects anonymized telemetry, such as:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device category (desktop, mobile, tablet).
  • Pages visited, time spent per page, and referral URLs.
  • Approximate geographical location at city or state level (IP addresses are anonymized).

This information cannot be used to personally identify individual visitors.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

StampDutyCalc.in is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website based on a user's prior visits to this site and other websites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their browsing history.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.

4. Communication & Contact Inquiries

If you contact us via email at admin@stampdutycalc.in, we receive your email address and any details provided in your correspondence. We use this information solely to answer your questions, resolve reported calculation discrepancies, or respond to partnership inquiries. We never sell or share your contact email with third-party telemarketers.

5. External Links & WhatsApp Interactions

Our website provides educational links to state government portals (e.g. wbregistration.gov.in, tnreginet.gov.in) and an optional WhatsApp contact shortcut. Clicking on an external link or initiating a chat on WhatsApp redirects you to an external platform governed by its own independent terms and privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6. Cookie Management in Your Browser

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can alter your browser settings to decline or delete cookies at any time. For detailed instructions on managing cookies, refer to your browser's documentation (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge).

7. Children’s Online Privacy

StampDutyCalc.in is an educational financial utility intended for adults, property buyers, and real estate professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or technology practices. The "Effective Date" at the top will indicate when the policy was last revised.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email our administrative team at: admin@stampdutycalc.in.
